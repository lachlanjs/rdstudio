# The ideas

From the developer, 2026-10-06, as given:

- **Arbo:** organic, tree themed (root systems, leaves, floral, etc.).
- **Carto:** cartographic map themed (grid, Google Maps like, hachures,
  etc.).
- **Sevasto:** Alien: Isolation themed (already done).
- **Termo:** ASCII terminal themed (text-grid based, ligatures, `tree`
  command-like).
- **Metro:** metropolitan subway map themed (bright colours, like the game
  Mini Metro).

# Where each stands

- **Sevasto** is the theme built as Station

- The other four are ideas only. Nothing is designed or built.

# Notes from the agent

Not the developer's words; things to weigh when one of these is taken up.

- A theme here is tokens and rules laid over Marginalia's, so each screen
  is built once ([Station's decision](/decisions/station-theme.md) rests on
  that). Arbo, Carto and Metro each suggest a different drawing of the Atlas
  as well as different colours, which is more than a restyle.
- **Carto** is close to the Survey direction sketched in the
  [redesign](/design/redesign.md), and to the hachures and contours of the
  continuous Atlas retired in [T63](/tasks/T63-grid-atlas-parity.md).
- **Metro** would use bright colours for lines. In Marginalia colour is
  meaning (the three pens), so Metro needs its own answer to what a colour
  says.
- **Termo** is close to the "terminal lean" of the design's brand book.
