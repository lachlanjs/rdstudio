### Solving Problems:

As discussed already at length. A core benefit of putting effort into our learning practices is that it enables us to solve problems of greater and greater difficulty, and of greater and greater impact. 

### The Boundaries of the Map

Now equipped with a deep understanding of the inner workings of our own projects and interests, we can look outward. We are in a better position to understand our map's boundary, and start extending it.

### Becoming Better Learners

Being a lifelong learner not only entails that you apply yourself to new domains, but that you also apply yourself to improving your ability to learn and the systems under which you do so.

As with any skill, practice makes us better. Improving our learning ability and strengthening our systems and techniques has the $N$-fold benefit to our $N$ different projects, interests, and areas of expertise.

### Improving our Mental Welfare

To enhance our systems of understanding is to also enhance the intimacy of our own relationship with our own knowledge. Humans hate uncertainty. It manifests in the emotions of unease and fear, which are unpleasant, and exacerbated by the pace at which we are building without understanding. Investing time into systematically catching up our understanding will help alleviate these adverse mental effects.
