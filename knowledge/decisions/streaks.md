# Decision

Show daily and weekly streaks for recall, new learning, problem solving and
all three ([design](/design/streaks.md)).

# Why

The developer asked for them: a reason to come back each day matters more
over a long course of study than the risks the rule guarded against.

# Conditions

- Only work that teaches counts: practising notes due, reaching new notes,
  answering exercises. Opening pages does not.
- An empty review queue is a rest day, not a miss.
- Reprieves, earned by keeping a streak, absorb an occasional missed day.
- Streaks never count as evidence of understanding.

# Reopen if

Streaks are seen to push volume over understanding: many shallow answers,
give-ups that turn into rushed attempts, or the load note shown often.
